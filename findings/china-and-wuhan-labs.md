# Chinese authorities and Wuhan laboratories

Status: documented access failures and attributed administrative findings.

## Chinese government and health authorities

WHO publicly criticised delayed disclosure of market-sample data in March 2023. It continued to request early infection sequences, animal information and laboratory records in 2025. These gaps obstruct investigation across competing origin hypotheses. [S17] [S08]

SAGO records that a WHO Secretariat letter asking for further investigations, audits, documentation and staff interviews at WIV and the Wuhan CDC laboratory was refused. It also records that a request to the China National Center for Bioinformation for 508 early-patient sequences had not been met by June 2025. [S07] China's National Health Commission responded that China had shared origins data "without reservation" and called some of SAGO's requests "groundless", as reported by CGTN. [S85] The [WHO dossier](who.md#outstanding-evidence-requests) itemises each request and the recorded response.

**Project recommendation:** publish the requested evidence with sufficient metadata for independent analysis; explain withholding decisions; permit independently verifiable access while protecting legitimate patient privacy.

These requests concern the conduct of authorities and institutions. They do not attribute collective responsibility to Chinese people or assume that all Chinese researchers controlled disclosure decisions.

## Wuhan Institute of Virology

HHS's September 2023 determination relies on NIH's finding that a WIV experiment had possibly exceeded the viral-growth limit permitted under the grant. It treats repeated failures to supply the original laboratory notebooks and electronic files requested to assess that compliance as an aggravating factor. It imposed a ten-year debarment, counting suspension time, through 16 July 2033. The determination was uncontested. [S14]

The original records and an independent compliance review remain legitimate demands. The administrative decision does not establish that WIV possessed SARS-CoV-2 before the outbreak or caused the pandemic.

The inspector general also records that WIV had cooperated with monitoring for several years before its post-outbreak lack of cooperation limited oversight. Include that distinction when describing the history. [S13]

## Other Wuhan laboratories

SAGO sought information relating to both WIV and the Wuhan CDC laboratory. A request for investigation is not itself a finding of wrongdoing. No separate adjudicated failure by another laboratory is established in this initial dossier. [S07]

Before adding a laboratory to an accountability claim, identify a specific obligation, decision, experiment or records refusal and its supporting evidence.
