# Proposed WHO acknowledgment and reform statement

**Project advocacy draft. WHO has not issued this statement.**

> The 2021 joint report described a laboratory incident as extremely unlikely without a sufficiently thorough investigation to justify that confidence. We are sorry for the overstatement and the confusion it created.
>
> Our director-general challenged the assessment at the time. We should explain that disagreement and our subsequent position more clearly. Available evidence favours zoonotic spillover, while missing information prevents an adequate assessment of a research-related origin.
>
> We will publish an account of how the earlier assessment was reached, how its limitations were communicated and what has changed. Future reports should distinguish unavailable evidence from evidence against a hypothesis, make uncertainty prominent and explain significant disagreement.
>
> We will report publicly on implementation of our investigation framework, outstanding data requests and unresolved constraints.

## Why this wording

The acknowledgment is a proposed institutional judgment, not a quotation or an established finding of misconduct. The underlying chronology and current assessment are documented in the [WHO dossier](../findings/who.md).

The draft recognises Tedros's contemporaneous objections. It asks WHO to account for its responsibility without pretending WHO can compel another government to provide records.

The proposed apology concerns confidence and communication. It does not claim that a laboratory origin has become the established explanation.

## Concrete follow-through requested

- Publish an accessible explanation alongside the 2021 report.
- Document approval of hypothesis rankings and how dissent was handled.
- Report which new investigation procedures have actually been implemented.
- Publish a dated register of outstanding evidence requests and responses.
- Explain how uncertainty is represented in headlines, summaries and media briefings.

These are proposed commitments. Do not present them as reforms WHO has already adopted.
