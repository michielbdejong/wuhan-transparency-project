Dates below are publication or decision dates unless stated otherwise. Review cutoff: 14 November 2026.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Chronology corrections that matter

A 2026 disclosure is not the first public disclosure of Proximal Origin Slack messages. Conversely, evidence of a 2023 release does not show that all later material is recycled.

WHO leadership's early objections cannot be explained as a response to the 2023 messages: the chronology rules that out.

A document's continued availability does not make it the current institutional position. Cite later assessments alongside historical findings.
